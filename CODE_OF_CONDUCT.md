# Code of Conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Welcome questions and constructive criticism.
Discuss technical decisions using evidence and acknowledge mistakes openly.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sharing private recordings or
credentials without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, open a
[contact request](https://github.com/aihohu/MomentAI/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
containing only “Please provide a private conduct-report contact.” Do not identify
participants or describe sensitive incidents publicly. Wait for the private channel
before providing relevant links, dates, and a concise description. Security
vulnerabilities use the separate [Security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or close abusive
threads. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the relevant behavior.
